Project Overview

Leaving the hospital often means patients have to manage multiple medications, follow-up appointments, and home-care instructions on their own. Discharge reports can be difficult to understand, and missing or misinterpreting instructions can make recovery more challenging.

CareBridge aims to simplify this process by transforming hospital discharge reports into clear, personalized post-discharge care plans.

What it aims to provide:

Simplified explanations of medical discharge instructions.

Organized medication schedules and reminders.

A structured calendar for medications and follow-up appointments.

Daily home-care checklists and recovery instructions.

Identification of unclear or missing information that requires confirmation from a healthcare professional.

Built with: AWS Strands Agents SDK and AWS services.

Goal: Help patients and caregivers better understand, organize, and follow their post-discharge care instructions.

Currently under development as part of the Strands SDK Workshop to Ship Hackathon.
